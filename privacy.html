Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Controller & contact

Pleasure Places is operated by Jiří Rýsavý (the "controller"). For any question about this policy or your personal data, contact support@pleasure-place.com.

Data we collect

To provide the app's core features, we collect: your email address, display name, and date of birth (to verify you meet the minimum age); optionally, your gender; a profile photo, if you choose to add one; the places you create, including their name, description, and GPS coordinates; photos you attach to places or activities; ratings and reviews you write; your pairing with a partner ("couple"); and a push-notification token if you enable notifications.

Why we process your data

We process this data to: create and run your account and couple pairing (performance of a contract); show places and challenges and let you and your partner interact with them (performance of a contract); send you password-reset, verification, and activity emails and push notifications (performance of a contract / legitimate interest); and keep the service secure, for example by rate-limiting abusive requests (legitimate interest). Optional fields, like gender, are processed only with your consent, given by choosing to fill them in.

Who we share data with

We use the following processors to run the app. They only process your data on our instructions: Supabase (database hosting and photo storage), Render (backend hosting), Resend (transactional emails — password reset, verification), and Google (Sign in with Google, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications, if you use those features).

How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is removed immediately. Places, activities, and photos that only you created are deleted with your account; content you shared with a partner as part of a couple may remain visible to them, without being linked to your identity, since it was already part of your shared history. Backups kept by our infrastructure providers may persist for a short additional period before being overwritten.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or export your data, to object to certain processing, and to erase your data. You can exercise the right to erasure at any time by deleting your account in Settings, or by emailing us. If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Czech data protection authority, the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (uoou.cz).

International transfers

Some of our processors may process data outside the European Economic Area (for example, in the United States). Where this happens, we rely on their standard contractual clauses or another lawful transfer mechanism to protect your data.
